How to play

  1. Get in pairs. One person is Pizza A; the other is Pizza B.
  2. Choose HIGH (higher pizza price) or LOW (lower pizza price) without showing your partner.
  3. Reveal at the same time and use the payoff matrix to see how many points each shop gets.
  4. Keep your points. Play 5 turns total: Round 1 = 1 turn, Round 2 = 1 turn, Round 3 = 3 turns.
  5. The highest total score wins!
Round 1: no talking.
Round 2: talk and make promises first (but your choices remain private).
Round 3: 3 turns with the same partner; try a repeated-game strategy such as Tit for Tat.

Example

Pizza A chooses LOW. Pizza B chooses HIGH.

A earns 8 points. B earns 1 point.

Why? A attracts more customers by charging less, so B loses customers.

Payoffs are fictional classroom points, not actual dollar profits. In the real world, price fixing and agreements between competitors can be illegal.

Try a strategy

Tit for Tat

Start HIGH, then copy your partner's previous choice.

Always Defect

Always choose LOW.

Always Cooperate

Always choose HIGH.

Tit for Two Tats

Choose LOW only after your partner chooses LOW twice in a row.

Win-Stay, Lose-Shift

Set a success rule (e.g., 5+ points). Repeat your choice when successful; otherwise switch.

Grim Trigger

Start HIGH. If your partner ever chooses LOW, choose LOW forever after.
